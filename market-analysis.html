📈 SaaS MARKET ANALYSIS - October 28, 2026

Market Overview
•Market size: The global SaaS market is projected at $530.0 billion in 2026, up from $464.7 billion in 2025, and is forecast to reach $1.109 trillion by 2033, representing an 11.1% CAGR.[3]
•B2B segment: B2B SaaS is estimated at $434.4 billion in 2026, growing to $931.5 billion by 2033 at an 11.5% CAGR.[5]
•Key growth drivers: Enterprise cloud migration, embedded and generative AI, workflow automation, cybersecurity, vertical software, and increased demand for recurring, usage-based technology spending.
•Geographic distribution: North America remains the largest market, accounting for 44.1% of global SaaS revenue in 2025 and 43.1% of B2B SaaS revenue.[3][5] Asia-Pacific is expected to be the fastest-growing major region, supported by digital transformation, expanding internet access, and rising enterprise-software adoption.[5][7]
•Investor implication: Market growth remains attractive, but category growth alone is insufficient; durable retention, efficient growth, and clear AI monetization increasingly determine returns.

Investment & Funding Trends
•Capital allocation: Funding is concentrating in AI-enabled cybersecurity, data infrastructure, developer tools, vertical SaaS, and products that automate high-value business processes.
•Valuation reset: The median public SaaS revenue multiple fell to approximately 3.4x EV/revenue by March 2026, while median private SaaS acquisition pricing was around 3.1x EV/revenue in Q1 2026.[8]
•Recovery with selectivity: The public SaaS index declined to 3.1x ARR in June before recovering to 4.6x by August 31, a roughly 48% rebound.[1]
•Growth-quality premium: Public SaaS companies growing 30–40% traded at a median 15.2x LTM revenue in Q2 2026, compared with 8.7x for 40–50% growers and 7.7x for companies growing above 50%.[2]
•Private M&A: Across 31 disclosed 2026 technology transactions, non-outlier deals averaged 6.2x EV/revenue; transactions valued at 20x or more averaged 29.3x, largely reflecting exceptional AI-related assets.[1]
•Stage benchmarks: Broad stage-specific 2026 averages were not available in the cited data. As practical market benchmarks, lower-middle-market SaaS companies trade near 4.5x ARR, strong performers near 7–9x, and exceptional businesses with Rule of 40 above 50 and net revenue retention above 120% can reach 10–12x.[9]
•Actionable insight: Founders should optimize for efficient, repeatable growth rather than maximum top-line expansion. Investors should distinguish AI-supported product improvements from businesses with defensible AI-driven pricing and retention.
