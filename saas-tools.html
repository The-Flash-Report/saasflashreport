🛠️ SaaS TOOL RECOMMENDATIONS - August 19, 2026

Here’s a current, concise guide built around recently updated and trending SaaS pricing and product changes.

Essential SaaS Tools for Startups

•CRM & Sales
•Pipedrive Lite — $14/user/month. Strong for lean sales teams with pipeline tracking, deal automation, and a lighter entry price than many competitors.[12]
•Close CRM Essentials — $49/seat/month. Best for high-velocity outbound sales, calling, email automation, and pipeline management; pricing was restructured in 2026.[12]
•Salesforce Starter Suite — $25/user/month. Good for startups that want a scalable CRM foundation with sales and service basics plus room to grow.[12]

•Marketing Automation
•MailerLite — Free to start; paid tiers begin around $9/month. Best for email campaigns, automation workflows, landing pages, and small-team simplicity.[10]
•Brevo — Free tier available; paid plans commonly start around $9/month. Strong for email + SMS + CRM-style automation in one platform.[3][10]
•ConvertKit — Free tier available; creator-focused paid plans typically begin around $15/month. Best for audience growth, forms, funnels, and simple automations.[3][10]

•Customer Support
•Freshdesk — $15–$79/month. Good for startup support teams that need ticketing, automations, and omnichannel support at a reasonable price.[15]
•Intercom — premium pricing, usually custom quote. Best for conversational support, AI-assisted help desks, and in-app messaging for fast-growing startups.[11]
•Help Scout — typically lower-cost than enterprise suites, with shared inboxes, knowledge base, and simple team workflows; best for small support teams.[4][10]

Enterprise SaaS Solutions

•Business Intelligence
•Looker — custom enterprise pricing. Strong for governed analytics, embedded BI, and deep model-driven reporting for large organizations.[14]
•Tableau — enterprise pricing varies. Best for visual analytics, broad self-service reporting, and executive dashboards at scale.[14]
•Power BI — lower entry cost than many enterprise BI suites, with advanced reporting and Microsoft ecosystem integration.[14]

•HR & Payroll
•Workday — custom quote. Best for enterprise HR, payroll, workforce planning, and finance-adjacent workflows.[14]
•Rippling — pricing is typically modular and quote-based at scale; strong for HR, payroll, IT, and employee lifecycle automation.[14]
•Gusto — lower-cost option for smaller teams and growing companies; payroll, benefits, onboarding, and compliance features.[10][14]

•Project Management
•Asana Advanced — $24.99/seat/month. Suitable for enterprise teams needing work management, automation, and reporting; pricing increased in 2026.[9]
•ClickUp AI — from $7/user/month, with AI as a separate add-on from $9/user/month. Good for teams wanting all-in-one task, docs, and AI workflow management.[11]
•Monday.com — generally priced for mid-market
