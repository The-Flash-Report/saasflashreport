Essential SaaS Tools for Startups



# CRM & Sales



•HubSpot CRM — Pricing: Free CRM; paid Sales Hub from approximately $20–$100/user/month, with enterprise pricing by quote. Key features: Contact management, pipelines, email tracking, automation, reporting, and broad integrations. Best for: Early-stage startups through scaling SMBs.
•Pipedrive — Pricing: From approximately $15/user/month; higher tiers add automation and advanced reporting. Key features: Visual sales pipelines, lead management, email synchronization, workflow automation, and forecasting. Best for: Small sales-led teams.
•Salesforce Starter — Pricing: Approximately $25/user/month. Key features: Lead and account management, opportunity tracking, customizable reports, and upgrade path to the full Salesforce platform.[8] Best for: Startups expecting complex sales processes or rapid growth.

# Marketing Automation



•MailerLite — Pricing: Free tier; paid plans from approximately $10/month, depending on subscriber volume. Key features: Email campaigns, landing pages, forms, automation, and basic analytics. Best for: Solo founders and small marketing teams.
•Brevo — Pricing: Free tier; paid plans from approximately $9/month, with pricing influenced by email volume. Key features: Email and SMS campaigns, transactional messaging, CRM, segmentation, and automation. Best for: Cost-conscious startups with multichannel campaigns.
•ActiveCampaign — Pricing: Typically from approximately $15–$49/month for small contact lists; advanced plans cost more. Key features: Email automation, behavioral segmentation, CRM, lead scoring, and customer journeys. Best for: Growing startups requiring sophisticated lifecycle automation.

# Customer Support



•Help Scout — Pricing: Free limited plan; paid plans from approximately $25/user/month.[2] Key features: Shared inboxes, knowledge bases, customer profiles, collision detection, and reporting. Best for: Small and midsize support teams.
•Freshdesk — Pricing: Free or low-cost entry plans may be available, while advanced tiers are generally priced per agent; verify the current quote before purchasing.[2] Key features: Ticketing, automation, knowledge base, omnichannel support, and service analytics. Best for: Startups moving from email-based support to a structured help desk.
•Intercom — Pricing: Public pricing can vary; a cheaper Essential seat has reportedly been tested at $19–$29.[6] Key features: In-app messaging, AI-assisted support, chat, help center, proactive campaigns, and customer-service workflows. Best for: Product-led startups with strong in-app engagement needs.

Enterprise SaaS Solutions



# Business Intelligence



•Microsoft Power BI — Pricing: Commonly starts around $10–$15/user/month for individual paid licenses; enterprise capacity is quoted separately. Key features: Self-service dashboards, Microsoft 365 integration, semantic models, governance, and embedded analytics. Best for: Midmarket and enterprise organizations standardized on Microsoft.
