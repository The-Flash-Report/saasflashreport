🛠️ SaaS TOOL RECOMMENDATIONS - November 04, 2026

*Pricing is generally monthly, per user unless stated otherwise. Enterprise pricing often requires a quote. Verify final pricing before purchase because SaaS prices are changing rapidly; 76.5% of tracked 2026 price moves were increases.[1]*

Essential SaaS Tools for Startups



# CRM & Sales



•HubSpot CRM — Price: Free CRM; paid Sales Hub from approximately $20/seat, with higher automation tiers quoted or substantially more expensive. Features: Contact management, deal pipelines, email tracking, forms, reporting, and broad integrations. Best for: Startups wanting a generous free foundation and an upgrade path. HubSpot Free supports unlimited contacts.[6]
•Freshsales — Price: Free plan for up to 3 users; paid plans from $19/user/month. Features: AI-assisted lead scoring, email sequences, pipelines, built-in phone, and sales reporting. Best for: Small and growing sales teams. Pricing was verified November 2, 2026.[2][6]
•Close — Price: From $19/user/month. Features: Built-in calling, SMS, email sequences, pipeline management, and sales automation. Best for: Founder-led sales and inside-sales teams. Note: Close removed its free tier.[2][6]

# Marketing Automation



•MailerLite — Price: Free tier; paid pricing varies by subscriber count. Features: Email campaigns, landing pages, automation workflows, forms, and basic websites. Best for: Early-stage companies prioritizing low operating cost. Caution: The free allowance was reduced to 250 subscribers in 2026.[7]
•GetResponse — Price: From $19/month. Features: Email automation, landing pages, webinars, segmentation, and conversion funnels. Best for: Small businesses needing more than basic newsletters. Pricing was verified November 2, 2026.[2]
•Kit — Price: Free tier; paid plans from $33/month. Features: Creator-focused email automation, tagging, broadcasts, landing pages, and subscriber monetization. Best for: Startups building an audience, newsletter, or creator-led acquisition channel.[2]

# Customer Support



•Help Scout — Price: Free plan; paid plans from $25/user/month. Features: Shared inboxes, knowledge base, customer profiles, reporting, and lightweight automation. Best for: Small support teams seeking a simple, human-centered help desk.[2]
•LiveChat — Price: From $25/user/month. Features: Website chat, routing, canned responses, analytics, chat history, and integrations. Best for: SaaS companies with real-time pre-sales or support needs.[2]
•Tidio — Price: Free tier; paid plans from approximately $24.17/month. Features: Live chat, chatbots, ticketing, automation, and AI-assisted replies. Best for: Small teams combining chat and basic automation.[2]
