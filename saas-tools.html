Essential SaaS Tools for Startups

•CRM & Sales
- HubSpot CRM — *Free*; paid tiers start at about $15/user/month for starter plans in some summaries, with broader HubSpot marketing/sales bundles starting higher; best for startups needing an all-in-one CRM and marketing stack with AI lead scoring and unified sales/marketing/service workflows.[3][6][16]
- Pipedrive — pricing not listed in the provided results; best for sales-focused startups that want pipeline visibility and activity-based selling.[6]
- Salesmate — $12/user/month starter pricing; includes automated follow-up emails, virtual phone system, and sales forecasting; good for small teams that want a lightweight CRM with built-in calling and automation.[11]

•Marketing Automation
- HubSpot Marketing Hub — starts at $45/month in the provided results, with higher Professional and Enterprise tiers; includes email campaigns, landing pages, lead scoring, and AI content assistance.[11][3]
- ActiveCampaign — pricing not listed in the provided results; commonly used for lifecycle email automation and segmentation, and included as a top marketing automation example in the results.[10]
- Mailchimp — pricing not listed in the provided results; suitable for startups needing email campaigns, basic segmentation, and simple automation.[10]

•Customer Support
- Zendesk — $19/month starting price in the provided results; offers multi-channel ticketing, AI ticket routing, and suggested replies.[7]
- Intercom — $74/month starting price in the provided results; includes unified chat, support, outreach, Fin AI chatbot, auto-resolution, and AI composer.[7]
- Freshdesk — pricing not listed in the provided results; cited as a customer support automation option with chatbots, ticket routing, FAQs, and SLA tracking.[10]
